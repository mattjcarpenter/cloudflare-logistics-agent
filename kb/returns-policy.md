# Returns policy

Customers can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The return window starts on the day the order was delivered, not the day it shipped.

Orders that were cancelled, or that have not been delivered yet, cannot be returned. If a package is held by the carrier because of a delivery exception, the customer should contact support instead of starting a return.

Items marked final sale, gift cards, and opened personal care products cannot be returned.

The customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the return.
