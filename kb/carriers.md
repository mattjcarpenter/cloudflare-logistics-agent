# Carriers

We ship with UPS, FedEx and USPS. The carrier is chosen by the warehouse based on package weight and destination; customers cannot pick a carrier.

All orders ship from our warehouse in Cumming, Georgia (zip 30040). We do not ship to Alaska or Hawaii.

Tracking numbers are shared once an order ships. UPS tracking numbers start with 1Z. FedEx tracking numbers are 12 digits. USPS tracking numbers are 22 digits.

If a carrier reports a delivery exception, such as an address that cannot be found, the package is held at the local carrier facility for up to 5 days before it is returned to the warehouse.
