# Shipping FAQ

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day are processed the same day. Orders placed later are processed the next business day.

We offer three services: ground (about 5 business days), two-day, and overnight. Prices depend on package weight and distance from the warehouse.

Customers can change the shipping address only while the order is still processing. Once an order ships, the address cannot be changed.

A processing order can be cancelled at no cost. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled; the customer can return the item after delivery instead.
